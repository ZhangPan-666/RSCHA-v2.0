# Mathematical Foundations of Revised Spherical Cap Harmonic Analysis

## November 17, 2026 edition

The handbook derives the four-family R-SCHA expansion on a spherical-cap
shell and treats the one-surface R-SCHA2D problem separately. It develops
boundary traces, convergence, field and tensor evaluation, and the energy
measures used for shell and surface spectra.

The LaTeX source is assembled from 24 sections in the order specified by
`main.tex`. With TeX Live on Windows, run `./build.ps1`. The basis and
derivative figures and their generation scripts are in `figures/`.

The November 6 audit and its numerical records are available in
`notes/handbook_audit_20261106.md` and `reproducibility/audit_20261106/`.
