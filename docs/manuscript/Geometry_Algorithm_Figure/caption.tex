Geometry and numerical architecture of the \RSCHA basis. (a) The shell $\Omega$ is bounded by $S_1$ ($r=r_1$), $S_2$ ($r=r_2$), and $\theta=\theta_0$; $O$ is the geocenter, $z_g$ is the geographic polar axis, and $P(r,\theta,\varphi)$ uses local cap coordinates. The geometry is schematic and not to scale. (b) Family-specific kernels preserve amplitude conventions and component-wise signed-log states. (c) Offline carrier-removed residuals or logarithmic norms are compressed into Chebyshev tables; online queries use admissible lookups or specified non-table paths. (d) The Legendre degree sensitivity $S=\partial_\nu L$ links root refinement to the full boundary identity for the squared cap norm $I$. Legendre point values use the Schmidt scale; Mehler and zero-eigenvalue point values include cap normalization. The Mehler Riccati identity requires no online Riccati integration.
